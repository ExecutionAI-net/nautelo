What we collect

Account data you give us (name, email, password stored only as a salted hash), listing content you publish, messages you send through the platform, and technical data needed to run and secure the service. Unique view counts use a keyed hash that cannot be reversed to an IP address; we do not store raw IP addresses for analytics.

Why we use it

To provide the marketplace, deliver messages and notifications, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising profiling.

Emails

We send service emails only: account verification, password reset, listing decisions, messages from other users and payment receipts. You can switch off notification emails in your account. Email is delivered by a transactional email provider acting on our behalf.

Photos

Images you upload are re-encoded and location and camera metadata (EXIF/GPS) is removed before they are shown publicly.

Payments

Card payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store card numbers.

Retention and your rights
